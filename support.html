Getting started
How do I add a book?

Tap the + button on the Library screen. You can search by title or author, scan the ISBN barcode, or add a book manually. Once found, choose a shelf, set your start date, and you're done.

What are shelves?

Shelves let you organise your library. Reading is for books you're currently reading, Finished for completed ones, Want to read for your to-read list, and Did not finish for books you abandoned.

How do I track my reading progress?

Open a book, go to the Info tab, and tap the page count to update your current page. Plume logs your daily reading and uses it to calculate streaks and stats.

Highlights
How do I add a highlight?

Open a book and go to the Highlights tab. Tap + Add highlight to type one manually, scan a page with your camera using OCR, or import from your e-reader.

How do I scan a highlight from a physical book?

In the Highlights tab, tap + Add highlight then choose Scan page. Point your camera at the text, and Plume will extract it automatically using on-device OCR. You can edit the result before saving.

Can I add a note to a highlight?

Yes. Open any highlight to edit it. There's a Your note section below the quote where you can write your thoughts. Notes are saved instantly.

Importing from e-readers
How do I export my highlights from Kindle?

Connect your Kindle to your Mac or PC via USB. Open it in Finder or File Explorer and look for the documents folder. Inside you'll find a file called My Clippings.txt. Copy it to your iPhone — via AirDrop, email, or Files — then open Plume, go to Profile → Import highlights, choose Kindle, and select the file.

How do I export my highlights from Kobo?

First, enable the export feature on your Kobo. Connect it to your Mac or PC via USB. On Mac, show hidden files with Cmd + Shift + . (period). Find the file at .kobo/Kobo/Kobo eReader.conf and open it in a text editor. Before the [General] section at the top, add two lines: [FeatureSettings] on one line and ExportHighlights=true on the next. Save, eject, and restart your Kobo. A new Export highlights option will appear when you press and hold on a book. Export each book individually — files are saved in the Exported Annotations folder at the root of your Kobo. Send the .txt files to your iPhone and import in Plume via Profile → Import highlights → Kobo.

What happens if a book already exists when I import?

Plume will detect the match and ask what to do. Merge adds the new highlights to the existing book. Replace overwrites the existing highlights. Add as new creates a separate entry.

Notes
What is the Notes tab on a book?

The Notes tab lets you write freeform thoughts about a book — things that don't belong on a specific quote. Think of it as a reading journal per book. Tap + Add note to create an entry. Notes are timestamped and saved automatically.

What's the difference between a highlight note and a book note?

A highlight note is a reaction tied to a specific quote. A book note is a standalone thought not linked to any passage — for broader reflections, questions, or ideas the book sparked.

Reflections
What are Reflections?

Reflections surface a past highlight from your library every week — a quiet moment to revisit something you found worth saving. When a reflection is ready, a subtle pill appears at the bottom of your Library. Tap it to read.

How does Plume choose which highlight to show?

Plume favours older highlights you haven't seen recently, so Reflections feel like rediscovery rather than repetition. The same highlight won't appear twice within six months.

Can I turn Reflections off?

Yes. Go to Profile → Settings and toggle Reflections off.

Stats
How are my reading stats calculated?

Stats are based on your reading log — updated each time you record page progress on a book. Books read, pages read, and streaks all come from this log, so keeping your progress updated gives you more accurate stats.

What is a reading streak?

A streak counts consecutive days where you met your daily page goal. Your current streak and best streak are shown on the Stats page.

How do I set a daily reading goal?

On the Stats page, tap Edit next to Today's goal. Set your target number of pages per day. Plume tracks whether you hit it each day.

iCloud sync
How does iCloud sync work?

When enabled, Plume syncs your library, highlights, notes, reads, and reading log to your private iCloud container. Your data is only visible to you — Plume never has access to it. Sync happens automatically when you open the app.

How do I enable iCloud sync?

Go to Profile → Settings and toggle iCloud sync on. Make sure you're signed into iCloud on your device. The first sync may take a moment depending on library size.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your device or in your private iCloud container. Plume has no backend servers and no access to your data. No tracking, no ads, no third-party sharing.

Data & backup
How do I back up my data?

Go to Profile → App data → Export backup. Plume creates a JSON file containing your entire library, highlights, and reading history. Save it somewhere safe — Files, iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself.

How do I restore from a backup?

Go to Profile → App data → Import backup and select your backup file. Plume will restore all your books, highlights, and reading history.

What happens if I delete all data?

Profile → App data → Delete all data permanently removes everything from your device and returns you to onboarding. This cannot be undone. If you have a backup or iCloud sync enabled, you can restore afterwards.
